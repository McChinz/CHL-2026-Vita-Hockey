# CHL 2026

Currently working on a be a pro style mode for forwards as well as goalies alongside an ultimate team mode that simulates the experience without being pay to win. 

Progress is at about 25% of where I want it, but full simulation is working with trades, call ups, injuries, and a full season with a playoff run in the game and working. Contracts control money earned from games, rest days let you train attributes, playing games gets you xp based on your actions and so on.

Be a Pro
- Start as a rookie, get drafted and play through 20 years in a fully simulated league with trades, contracts, injuries, call ups and line based gameplay. Earn XP through play and spend money earned each game based on your currently signed contract on new sticks, gear and other things that can influence your play. Don’t like your team? Request a trade. Living it up on the Avalanche and don’t want to be traded? Request a no trade clause. The world is your oyster
